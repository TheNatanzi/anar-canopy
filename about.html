How we work · روش کار ما

Five steps, one line

  1. Inquiry

    Write to us, or call. Tell us about your family and your dates.

  2. Consultation

    We meet your family, greet the elders first, and listen. Parents and elders are involved at the level the couple chooses.

  3. Proposal

    The plan, the price, and what is included: the events covered, the staffing, the hours and the fees. A page for the parents, in their language.

  4. Planning

    Ritual by ritual, as your family keeps it. A short written summary after every meeting.

  5. The wedding days

    The ceremonies your family chooses, in the order agreed with your family. One timeline, one person to call.
