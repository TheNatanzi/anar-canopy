The celebrations

Sofreh Aghd

سفرهٔ عقد

Sofreh Aghd

The Persian ceremony at the spread: the mirror, the candlesticks, the sugar above. We brief your sofreh designer and check the spread against the sofreh key (a one-page guide naming each item on the spread, in both scripts) before the elders are seated.

Nikah

نکاح

Nikah

The Islamic marriage contract, read before witnesses and both families. Quiet, exact, unhurried.

Mehndi & Sangeet

حنابندان

Mehndi & Sangeet

The mehndi, the henna night. The sangeet, when both families sing and dance. The baraat, arriving with music. Each run to the minute.

Haldi, Pheras & Anand Karaj

عروسی هندو و سیک

Haldi, Pheras & Anand Karaj

The haldi, the pheras around the sacred fire under the mandap, or the Anand Karaj before the Guru Granth Sahib. We cue the pandit or the granthi and both families, and hold the elders' seats. A one-line programme explains each ritual so every guest can follow.

How we work

01 · برنامه

Plan

We ask how your family observes each tradition. Then one plan with the price and what is included, Persian and Dari pages for the parents, and a written summary after every meeting.

02 · روز عروسی

Run the day

The timeline called with the venue and every vendor. Elders' seats held, the sofreh checked against the key, staff easy to find. You only have to be there.

  • Full planning

    Begins at the first conversation · ends at the thank-you

    One written plan with the rituals in order, the days, the price and what is included, with Persian and Dari pages for the parents. Planning meetings ritual by ritual, with a short written summary after each one. Every wedding day run: one timeline, called with the venue and every vendor.

  • Wedding-day coordination

    Begins 6–8 weeks out · we take over your plan and run the days

    Every contract, contact and time in your plan read and confirmed with the venue and every vendor. One timeline for every day, with the sofreh key or the programme checked against your family's wishes. On the days: elders greeted first, the officiant and family cued, our staff easy to find.
