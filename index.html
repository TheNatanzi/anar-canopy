The celebrations

These are examples; we confirm your family's customs and sequence with you and your officiant. Two heritages, different levels of observance, or celebrations not listed here? Tell us what your family keeps.

Sofreh Aghd

سفرهٔ عقد

Sofreh Aghd

The Persian ceremony at the spread: the mirror, the candlesticks, the sugar cones rubbed over the couple.

Read more

We brief your sofreh designer and check the spread against the sofreh key (a one-page guide naming each item on the spread, in the languages listed in your proposal) before the elders are seated.

Nikah

نکاح

Nikah

The Islamic marriage contract, read before witnesses and both families. Quiet, exact, unhurried.

Read more

If your family observes the aina mosaf (آینه‌مصحف), as many Afghan families do, it has its own moment, at the nikah or at the reception under the shawl, as your family keeps it: the couple's first look in the mirror, the Quran open between them, the elders close.

Mehndi & Sangeet

حنابندان · مهندی

Mehndi & Sangeet

Henna on the bride's hands and the elders' blessing one night; both families singing and dancing the next, as your family keeps it.

Read more

The henna gathering: hana-bandan for Persian families, khina for Afghan families, mehndi for South Asian families, with the henna on the bride's hands and the elders' blessing as your family keeps it. The sangeet, when both families sing, and the dholki or jaggo nights before it, if your family holds them. Music, dancing and the baraat on the wedding day, if your family chooses. Timing agreed around your family's customs.

Haldi & Pheras

Haldi & Pheras

The pheras under the mandap, with the pandit: your ceremony, in the sequence agreed with your officiant.

Read more

If your family observes the haldi, the turmeric before the wedding. If your family observes the baraat and the milni, we call them in the order of the agreed programme. We coordinate timing with the pandit and the venue, hold the elders' seats, and a one-line programme explains each ritual so every guest can follow.

Anand Karaj

Anand Karaj

The Sikh ceremony before the Guru Granth Sahib: the four laavan as the hymns are sung.

Read more

In the sequence agreed with your family and the granthi. If your family observes the baraat and the milni, we call them in the order of the agreed programme. We coordinate timing with the gurdwara, hold the elders' seats, and a one-line programme explains each ritual and what the gurdwara asks of guests.

Reception & Walima

جشن عروسی

Reception & Walima

The walima, the groom's family's feast after the nikah, and the reception, on one night or two as your family keeps it. We run each your family's way.

Read more

The elders' blessing, the entrances, the dancing if your family wishes: one timeline, called with the venue and every vendor, in the order agreed with your family.
