Two ways to work with us

One plan. One person to call.

  • Full planning

    Begins once your proposal is signed · the first conversation is free

    • Your budget tracked, line by line, once planning begins.
    • Venue and vendor shortlist, booking support, and contract review covering planning requirements and vendor logistics.
    • Planning decisions scheduled ritual by ritual, with a short written summary after each meeting.
    • One written plan: the rituals in order, the days, the price and what is included, with a page for the parents in Persian, Dari, Pashto, Urdu, Hindi, Punjabi or the language your parents read, agreed in your proposal.

    • Every contract, contact and time in it read and confirmed with the venue and every vendor.
    • One timeline for every day, with the sofreh key or the programme checked against your family's wishes.
    • On the days: elders greeted first, the officiant and family cued, our staff easy to find. You only have to be there.

The five steps

  1. 01 · Inquiry

    Write to us, or call.

    درخواست

    Tell us about your family and your dates. We reply within one business day.

  2. 02 · Consultation

    First, we hear your priorities.

    مشاوره

    We meet your family, greet the elders first, and listen. We ask how your family observes each tradition. Parents and elders included at the level you choose: the couple tells us who joins each meeting and how much the parents decide, and we keep to it.

  3. 03 · Proposal

    Here is the plan, the price, and what is included.

    پیشنهاد

    A short proposal with the rituals in order, the days, the price and exactly what is included: the agreed scope names the events covered, the staffing, the hours and the fees, before anything is booked. Together, we agree who approves decisions, who approves spending, and which updates each family receives. A page for the parents, in Persian, Dari, Pashto, Urdu, Hindi, Punjabi or the language your parents read, agreed in your proposal.

    A signed proposal and the deposit hold your dates; an inquiry or consultation does not.

  4. 04 · Planning

    Ritual by ritual, as your family keeps it.

    برنامه‌ریزی

    We plan each ceremony as your family keeps it: the ceremonies your family chooses, in the order agreed with your family. A short written summary follows every meeting, in English, with the page for the parents in the languages listed in your proposal. Parents and elders included at the level you choose.

  5. 05 · The wedding days

    Your ceremonies are ready. Your family is welcomed.

    روزهای عروسی

    One timeline, called with the venue and every vendor. Elders' seats held, the officiant and family cued, every sign coordinated with your printer and the venue in the languages listed in your proposal. When a sofreh is chosen, for example, the spread is checked against the sofreh key (a one-page guide naming each item on the spread, in those languages) before the elders are seated. Our staff are easy to find for the events and hours listed in your proposal. You only have to be there. Afterwards, as listed in your proposal: the thank-you card that follows, the files handed over, and one last call to close the plan.

Questions families ask

Seven answers

Our two families come from different cultures. Can you plan both?

Yes. Persian, Afghan and South Asian weddings are what we do, and mixed-heritage couples are welcome. We blend cultures across a multi-day celebration without diluting either: each ritual stays as that family keeps it.

Do you plan the whole wedding, or only run the day?

Both. Full planning begins once your proposal is signed and runs to the thank-you; the first conversation is free. Wedding-day coordination begins 6–8 weeks out: you have planned already, we run the days you have planned. We say which fits on the first call, with the price and what is included.

How early should we write?

As soon as you have a season in mind. Multi-day weddings fill venues and vendors early, and the best dates near Nowruz go first. Writing early costs nothing; we reply within one business day.

Will you work with our parents directly?

Yes. Parents and elders included at the level you choose. Elders are greeted first and asked, not told. The couple sets the level of parent involvement, and the proposal has a page for the parents in Persian, Dari, Pashto, Urdu, Hindi, Punjabi or the language your parents read, agreed in your proposal.

Which languages do you plan in?

Planning runs in English. Written pages for the parents are translated into the language you name. For calls and meetings in another language, tell us which one; we confirm who speaks it before booking.

Where do you work?

The San Francisco Bay Area, from the city to the East Bay and the Peninsula. For a wedding further afield, write to us and we will tell you plainly whether we can.

What does it cost?

Every proposal is priced to the events, days and staffing it covers. Full planning and wedding-day coordination are quoted after the first conversation.
