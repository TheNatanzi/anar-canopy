Five steps on one line

How we work

روش کار ما

One plan, one person to call, nothing to chase. We plan with your family, ritual by ritual, and we run the day.

The five steps

  1. 01 · Enquiry & consultation

    Thank you for writing. Tell us about your family.

    درخواست و مشاوره

    Write to us, or call. We reply within one business day, then meet your family. Parents and elders included at the level you choose. We greet the elders first, ask how your family observes each tradition, and listen.

  2. 02 · Proposal

    Here is the plan, the price, and what is included.

    پیشنهاد

    A short proposal with the rituals in order, the days, the price and exactly what is included. One page is written for the parents, in Persian or Dari, so they can read it alone.

  3. 03 · Planning

    Ritual by ritual, as your family keeps it.

    برنامه‌ریزی

    We plan each ceremony as your family keeps it, from the engagement to the walima: aghd or nikah, mehndi and sangeet, reception. Your deposit holds the date. A short written summary follows every meeting, in English, with Persian and Dari pages for the parents. Parents and elders included at the level you choose.

  4. 04 · The wedding days

    The sofreh is ready. Your grandparents are seated.

    روزهای عروسی

    One timeline, called with the venue and every vendor. Elders' seats held, the officiant and family cued, the sofreh checked against the sofreh key (a one-page guide naming each item on the spread, in both scripts), every sign in both scripts coordinated with your printer and the venue. Our staff are easy to find. You only have to be there.

  5. 05 · Thank-you

    It was an honour to plan this with your family.

    سپاس

    What the family keeps, and how we say goodbye: the thank-you card that follows, the files handed over, and one last call to close the plan.

Questions families ask

Six answers

Our two families come from different cultures. Can you plan both?

Yes. Persian, Afghan and South Asian weddings are what we do, and mixed couples are many of our families. We blend cultures across a multi-day celebration without diluting either: each ritual stays as that family keeps it.

Do you plan the whole wedding, or only run the day?

Both. Full planning runs from the first conversation to the thank-you. Wedding-day coordination begins 6–8 weeks out: you have planned already, we take over your plan and run the days. We say which fits on the first call, with the price and what is included.

How early should we write?

As soon as you have a season in mind. Multi-day weddings fill venues and vendors early, and the best dates near Nowruz go first. Writing early costs nothing; we reply within one business day.

Will you work with our parents directly?

Yes. Parents and elders included at the level you choose. Elders are greeted first and asked, not told, and the proposal has a page for the parents in Persian or Dari.

Which languages do you plan in?

Persian and Dari pages for the parents; English for everyone. The plan and the programme are written in English; the parents' pages come in Persian or Dari, with Persian digits where they belong. Say which you want in the form, and we reply in that language.

Where do you work?

The San Francisco Bay Area, from the city to the East Bay and the Peninsula. For a wedding further afield, write to us and we will tell you plainly whether we can.
