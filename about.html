Our celebrations are built on centuries of poetry, hospitality and sacred tradition. Why I started Anar

What anar means

Anar is the pomegranate: hundreds of seeds, held in one fruit. It sits on the sofreh aghd and on the Yalda table. For our families, it means family and plenty.

Honoring the roots. Elevating the vibe.
