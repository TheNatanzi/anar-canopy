Five steps on one line

How we work

روش کار ما

One plan, one person to call, nothing to chase. We plan with your family, ritual by ritual, and we run the day.

The five steps

  1. 01 · Inquiry

    Write to us, or call.

    درخواست

    Tell us about your family and your dates. We reply within one business day.

  2. 02 · Consultation

    First, we hear your priorities.

    مشاوره

    We meet your family, greet the elders first, and listen. We ask how your family observes each tradition. Parents and elders included at the level you choose: the couple tells us who joins each meeting and how much the parents decide, and we keep to it.

  3. 03 · Proposal

    Here is the plan, the price, and what is included.

    پیشنهاد

    A short proposal with the rituals in order, the days, the price and exactly what is included: the agreed scope names the events covered, the staffing, the hours and the fees, before anything is booked. Together, we agree who approves decisions, who approves spending, and which updates each family receives. A page for the parents, in Persian, Dari, Pashto, Urdu, Hindi or Punjabi. Translated summaries and spoken language support are agreed before booking and listed in your proposal.

  4. 04 · Planning

    Ritual by ritual, as your family keeps it.

    برنامه‌ریزی

    We plan each ceremony as your family keeps it: the ceremonies your family chooses, in the order agreed with your family. Your deposit holds the date. A short written summary follows every meeting, in English, with the page for the parents in their language. Parents and elders included at the level you choose.

  5. 05 · The wedding days

    The sofreh is ready. Your grandparents are seated.

    روزهای عروسی

    One timeline, called with the venue and every vendor. Elders' seats held, the officiant and family cued, the sofreh checked against the sofreh key (a one-page guide naming each item on the spread, in the languages listed in your proposal), every sign coordinated with your printer and the venue in those languages. Our staff are easy to find for the events and hours listed in your proposal. You only have to be there. Afterwards, as listed in your proposal: the thank-you card that follows, the files handed over, and one last call to close the plan.

Questions families ask

Six answers

Our two families come from different cultures. Can you plan both?

Yes. Persian, Afghan and South Asian weddings are what we do, and mixed couples are many of our families. We blend cultures across a multi-day celebration without diluting either: each ritual stays as that family keeps it.

Do you plan the whole wedding, or only run the day?

Both. Full planning runs from the first conversation to the thank-you. Wedding-day coordination begins 6–8 weeks out: you have planned already, we take over your plan and run the days. We say which fits on the first call, with the price and what is included.

How early should we write?

As soon as you have a season in mind. Multi-day weddings fill venues and vendors early, and the best dates near Nowruz go first. Writing early costs nothing; we reply within one business day.

Will you work with our parents directly?

Yes. Parents and elders included at the level you choose. Elders are greeted first and asked, not told. The couple sets the level of parent involvement, and the proposal has a page for the parents in Persian, Dari, Pashto, Urdu, Hindi or Punjabi.

Which languages do you plan in?

English for everyone. A page for the parents, in Persian, Dari, Pashto, Urdu, Hindi or Punjabi. Translated summaries and spoken language support are agreed before booking and listed in your proposal. The plan and the programme are written in English, with the languages agreed in your proposal alongside. Say your preferred language in the form.

Where do you work?

The San Francisco Bay Area, from the city to the East Bay and the Peninsula. For a wedding further afield, write to us and we will tell you plainly whether we can.
