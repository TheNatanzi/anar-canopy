# Benchmark, condensed (64 planner sites)

**The one rule:** the best sites say who they serve in the headline, name their process, show "starting at" prices, prove it with reviews, and end with a real inquiry form. Anar's site must do all five.

## The 7 strongest references

Scores are structure + messaging + copy out of 30. Steal the pattern, not the words.

| Site | Score | The ONE thing to steal |
|---|---|---|
| [Calder Clark](https://www.calderclark.com/offerings) | 27 | One service split into 3 named pillars; price discussed on a call |

## Gaps Anar can own

- **Say "Afghan" first.** No Afghan-branded planner site exists anywhere; rivals bury it in a FAQ or on WeddingWire.
- **Explain the traditions in plain words.** Only two of 64 sites walk through a ceremony; none explains Mehr.
- **Use Farsi/Dari script.** Every Persian peer writes Farsi in English letters only.
- **Talk to parents.** No site addresses the people who host and pay; add a parents page and a "You are..." field.
- **Combine all three cultures plus mixed couples.** Persian sites ignore Afghan and South Asian; Tamtastic ignores Persian.
