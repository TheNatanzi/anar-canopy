Buttons: six ways to say “begin”

Each concept in Primary and Ghost form, on both palettes, with its hover (or pressed) state rendered beside it. Labels are fixed: Begin the conversation (primary) and Inquire (ghost). TT Ramillas Bold for Latin, Noto Naskh Arabic for Persian. No gradients, no shadow heavier than 2px.

Shared tokensLatin label 15px TT Ramillas Bold (Seed: 12.5px caps, .16em); Persian 12px Noto Naskh Arabic; button height 52–58px; rules 1px or 2px; square corners except Arch. Night primary = gold on cypress, hover flips to parchment. Cream primary = red on cream, hover flips to brown.
ImplementationAll states are live CSS (:hover, :active, :focus-visible); the static copies on this sheet use .is-hover / .is-active. Classes: .btn.b-kashida, .b-arch, .b-seal, .b-seed, .b-bi, .b-press + .primary or .ghost, inside a .night or .cream scope.
